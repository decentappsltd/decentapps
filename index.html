Pi Webinars

Pi Webinars is one of the first dedicated DeEd (Decentralised Education) platforms and the winner of an Honourable Mention in the 2021 Pi Hackathon. Our aim is to make online education accessible to everyone, with the aid of blockchain.

How it Works:


Users can upload their videos to the Pi Webinars platform and receive Pi everytime their content is purchased. The more purchases, the more Pi you earn. Content can be formalised into courses to share knowledge with the world.
